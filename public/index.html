How the pool pays out

EVERY 48H

The pool is funded by rewards from project partners. Every two days, 1% of the total pool is drawn among six winners, split unevenly.

Nobody deposits anything to enter. Entries are reviewed for quality, not counted by volume — one sharp take outweighs ten generic ones.
